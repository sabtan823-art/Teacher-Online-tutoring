FAQ

Questions parents ask us

How does the free assessment work?

We evaluate your child's actual level through a short diagnostic session, then send a written roadmap showing their gaps and a realistic timeline. Free, no obligation.

What platform do you teach on?

Zoom or Google Meet — whichever works best for you. You'll get a calendar invite before each session.

How long is each session?

45–60 minutes, one-on-one with the same tutor each time.

How many sessions are included per month?

20 sessions per month, roughly 5 per week depending on scheduling.

Can we try a class before committing?

Yes — the free assessment is itself a live session, so you get a feel for the tutor before deciding.

How will I know my child is progressing?

You'll get direct WhatsApp or email updates from the tutor after key sessions. A full Parent Dashboard is in development.

What if my child needs a different curriculum?

We teach to your child's actual syllabus — UK, US, Canadian, IGCSE, O/A Level, Cambridge, or Edexcel. Just mention their school/board when booking.

Is there a contract?

No. Simple monthly plan, cancel anytime.

Who will actually teach my child?

Qualified tutors across all subjects — Math, Chemistry, Biology, Science, English, Computer Science, Physics — each with a minimum Master's, M.Phil, or PhD. Male and female tutors available. All experienced, patient, and results-focused.

What if my child is significantly behind grade level?

That's what our catch-up programs are for — like Math Foundations Recovery or O Level Chemistry Catch-Up — targeted plans with a clear goal, not just "more tutoring."

What if we're not satisfied with the tutor?

Just tell us — we'll switch your child to a different tutor at no extra cost, no hassle.

How do I reach you with a question or issue?

WhatsApp is fastest — usually a reply within a few hours. The contact form works too, for less urgent things.
